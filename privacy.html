Privacy Policy for GetDinefit

Effective Date: September 2026

GetDinefit ("we", "our", or "us") provides a nutritional tracking and restaurant modification application. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name and email address when you register using Supabase authentication.
  • Health and Fitness Data: Body weight, height, age, sex, and daily caloric/macronutrient targets you enter to calculate your personalized fuel gauge.
  • App Usage: Meals logged, items saved to your vault, and push notification tokens.
  • Purchase History: Transaction identifiers processed securely via Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. We never store credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to calculate your daily macro goals, display restaurant modifications, sync your saved vault across sessions, and send meal reminders.

3. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete your account and associated records directly inside the app under Profile > Delete Account or by emailing getdienfit@gmail.com. Upon request, all profile and macro tracking records will be permanently removed.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this policy, contact us at: getdienfit@gmail.com
